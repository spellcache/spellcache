## What it does

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Collection" icon="open-book">
    Every copy with its finish, condition and language. Binders, lists, and
    fast search and filters over the whole catalogue.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Decks" icon="list-format">
    Plan from what you own or not, check Commander legality, assemble from
    owned copies, dismantle back.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Value" icon="seti:graphql">
    Daily TCGplayer ($) or Cardmarket (€) prices per collection, binder and
    deck.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Trading" icon="random">
    A step-by-step wizard, in person or live with another account of your
    server, that updates your collection when the trade is done.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Sharing" icon="add-document">
    Collections shared between accounts with Owner, Contributor and Guest
    roles. Public links for decks and binders.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Mobile first" icon="laptop">
    Installable as an app, Android APK on every release, sidebar and preview
    pane on large screens.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
